Our Mission

Where Coffee Meets Conversation.

"The Social was born out of a simple need: a quiet corner for the creators, a warm hub for the friends, and the most artisanal brew in the heart of the city."

We source our beans directly from single-origin estates in Chickmagalur, roasting them in small batches to preserve every botanical note. Our space is architected with natural textures, ergonomic nooks, and high-speed fiber to ensure your creativity flows as freely as our espresso.
