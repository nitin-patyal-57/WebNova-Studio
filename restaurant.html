The Alchemy
of Spice.

"Our spices are not bought from shelves; they are curated from the fields of Kerala and Kashmir, hand-pounded by artisans who understand that a spice is a living soul."

Original Copper

Every curry is prepared in tinned copper Handis to maintain mineral integrity and even heat distribution.

Slow Charcoal

We use specific woods to infuse smoke—Kikar for kebabs and Mango wood for the earthen oven.

Aged Basmati

Our rice is aged for 24 months in a temperature-controlled environment for maximum aroma.
